Drive Bridge – Privacy Policy

Drive Bridge is used only by the CBNI research lab to upload the lab's own files to the lab's own Google Drive.

It uses Google Drive access only to check folders, upload files, and confirm the uploaded files match the originals. It does not delete or edit existing files.

No personal data is collected, sold, or shared with anyone. Login details are stored only on the lab's own computer.
